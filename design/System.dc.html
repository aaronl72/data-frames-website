30+ years hands-on

The live feed is the most distinctive thing on your current site, and it only appears once. Borrowing its language — mono type, status tags, the pulsing dot — for section markers, figures and pricing labels gives the whole page one voice instead of nine unrelated card grids.

Replacing the stock photography

The five industry photographs are generic stock, loaded straight from Unsplash's servers on every visit. Drawn vignettes in your own stroke style cost nothing to load, can't disappear, don't tell a third party who visits you — and look deliberate rather than bought.
