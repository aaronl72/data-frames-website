The serif goes soft below 32px. A favicon would need its own drawing.

Why
The conservative one, done properly. Initials set in your display serif inside a ruled square — a publisher's colophon rather than a tech logo. It signals an established practice, which is worth something when you are selling security to cautious owners.

Tradeoff
Typographic, not drawn, so it says nothing about what you do — and a lettered mark is the most common thing a small firm lands on. Weakest at small sizes of the four.
