Wide and short, so it sits low in a square avatar slot.

Why
The watching idea from L, without the animal. A solid lens with a square pupil — square, because this is a machine watching, not a person. It carries the same meaning the owl did and none of the risk of looking cute.

Tradeoff
An eye is a common mark, and in security it can read as surveillance — the thing your clients are afraid of, rather than the thing protecting them. Worth deciding whether that reading bothers you.
