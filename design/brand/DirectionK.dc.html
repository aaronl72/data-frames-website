Two shapes and a gap. There is nothing here that can break.

Why
The hardest and simplest graphic of the eight — a solid block with a fault line driven through it. No detail to lose, no thin strokes, and it reads from across a room or at 16px. It says you find the break, rather than that you own a padlock.

Tradeoff
It is pure abstraction. On its own it tells a stranger nothing about what you do, so it leans entirely on the name sitting next to it and on being used with real confidence.
