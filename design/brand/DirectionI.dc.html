Three solid slabs. Nothing to lose at any size.

Why
The data half of the name, at full weight. Three rows; one of them does not line up with the others. That is what an anomaly actually looks like on a screen, and it is the only mark here that shows the job rather than the tool.

Tradeoff
Three stacked bars is also the universal “menu” icon. The offset row is the whole defence against that reading, so it can never be softened.
