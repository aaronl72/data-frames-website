The descriptor has to come off below about 130px — it fills in.

Why
Nothing on the site tells a stranger what you sell in the first half second, and the logo is the one thing that appears on every surface. This puts the answer in the mark: the name in your display serif, and a tracked mono line beneath it, set to the exact width of the name so the pair reads as one object rather than a caption. It is the version that actually earns its place on a business card, a slide and a proposal cover, where the name alone leaves the reader guessing.

Tradeoff
Two-part lockups always need a one-part version, so you end up maintaining both and the descriptor gets dropped exactly where it would have helped most — small sizes. The words are also a commitment: “Security Operations” is the positioning for now, and if the offer widens the logo is what has to be reissued.
