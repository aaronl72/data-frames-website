Drop the line of type and the mark keeps going down to favicon size.

Why
Every other board in this round carries the same complaint in its tradeoff: no square version. This is the one that fixes it. Two letters, tracked until they almost touch, with the full name underneath doing the explaining — and when the space is too small for the name, the name comes off and the monogram survives alone. It is the only mark here that works at 32px, on an app icon and stitched into a shirt without being redrawn.

Tradeoff
DF is not yours. Two-letter monograms in bold sans are the most crowded space in identity design, and yours would sit near a dozen unrelated firms. It also says nothing — it has no idea inside it, and it needs the name beneath it to mean anything at all for the first year.
