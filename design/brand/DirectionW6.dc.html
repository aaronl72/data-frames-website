Mono holds its shape small better than anything else here.

Why
Your domain, set the way it would be typed, with a cursor still blinking after it. Lowercase and monospaced says technical without saying it out loud, and it is the only option here that reads as though the people behind it actually work in a terminal. Mono also stays legible at sizes that kill the serif.

Tradeoff
Terminal-styled logos are common enough in security to be their own cliché, and lowercase reads as informal — possibly too informal for owners deciding whether to trust you with their network. The cursor also implies motion it will never have on paper.
