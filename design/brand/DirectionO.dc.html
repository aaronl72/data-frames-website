Three flat blocks. It only ever gets simpler as it shrinks.

Why
Frames, plural — the part of your name none of the other marks use. Three of them stepping back into depth, which is also how layered defence actually works: nothing relies on one wall. Flat colour, no perspective tricks, no gradients.

Tradeoff
Stacked offset squares is the universal “copy” or “duplicate” icon, and some people will read it that way first. The alternating colour is what pulls it away from that, so it cannot go single-colour.
