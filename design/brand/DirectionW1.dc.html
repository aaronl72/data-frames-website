Closer to square than any single-line wordmark here, but still wider than tall.

Why
The name is the logo, and the frame is the picture frame from your brief — so the border is doing real work rather than decorating. Far more compact than a single-line wordmark, so it fits places a wide logo cannot Nearly square in proportion, which is the thing most wordmarks cannot do: this one will sit in a profile picture.mdash; though it is still wider than tall, so an avatar would want a cropped version.

Tradeoff
A boxed wordmark is a fussy thing to place — it fights any other rule or edge near it, and it needs clear space on all four sides to breathe. It is also the least flexible option at very small sizes.
