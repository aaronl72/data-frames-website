The D is a finished app icon on its own — nothing to redraw.

Why
W4 put the data in the hyphen. This puts it inside the D — the letter drawn rather than typed, its bowl opened up so three bars can stand in the counter. The mark is not attached to the name, it is the first letter of it, so there is no lockup to police and no clear-space rule to break. And the D detaches cleanly: it is a square, finished app icon at 32px without a redraw, which is the gap the rest of this round keeps admitting to.

Tradeoff
A custom first letter means the wordmark can never be typed — every use is an asset someone has to find the right file for, and the drawn D will never match IBM Plex exactly next to it. Bar charts in letterforms are also well-trodden ground in data companies, and this is a security firm, so the graph inside the D is arguably telling the wrong story about the work.
