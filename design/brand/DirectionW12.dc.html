Wide tracking is what saves it small — the letters never touch.

Why
The name with nothing done to it except space. Caps, monospaced, tracked out to the width of a caption — the way a model number is set on the back of a piece of equipment, or a title on an architectural drawing. It is the most restrained mark of the sixteen and the most confident, because it has nowhere to hide. Wide tracking also means the letters never collide, so it survives small better than any of the tight-set options.

Tradeoff
Restraint reads as absence to a lot of people, and this is the board most likely to be mistaken for a placeholder — the same objection that killed the quiet options in the first round. It is also very wide, which makes it the worst fit of the sixteen for a narrow header, and it has no square form at all.
