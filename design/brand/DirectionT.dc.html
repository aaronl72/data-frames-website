The bands vanish at 16px, but the silhouette survives.

Why
The most literal answer to the brief: a picture frame, and hung inside it the icon everyone already reads as data — the stacked database cylinder. Nobody has to be told what it means, and the round form against the square frame gives it a shape contrast the others do not have.

Tradeoff
A database cylinder is IT-infrastructure language rather than security, so it may say “we store your data” instead of “we defend it”. It is also the only mark here with curves, which makes it the odd one out beside your very square site.
