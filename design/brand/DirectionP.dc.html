Why
A signal cut clean through a solid block, entering one side and leaving the other — the monitoring never stops, it just passes through. Square steps rather than a smooth wave, because this is logged data, not a heartbeat.

Tradeoff
The busiest of the four. Four steps in a small tile is a lot of information, and it is the one most likely to look fussy on a business card or embroidered on a shirt.
