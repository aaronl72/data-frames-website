The hairline under it is the first thing to disappear in print.

Why
Newspaper masthead logic: the name in your display serif, pinned between a heavy rule and a hairline. The two different weights are what make it look considered rather than boxed in, and it carries the editorial character of the site straight into the logo.

Tradeoff
It reads as a publication rather than a security firm, and rules that thin are unreliable — they drop out in print, on screens and in embroidery. It also has no square version at all.
