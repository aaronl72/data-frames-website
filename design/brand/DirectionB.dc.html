Three rules inside a 16px box. They start to close up.

Why
The name read the other way: a data frame is a table. Header rule, two rows, one of them flagged green — the whole job in one glyph. Of the four, this is the one an analyst recognises instantly.

Tradeoff
The busiest mark here. Excellent at 32px and up, adequate at 16px, and it would not take embroidery or a single-colour stamp as cleanly as A.
