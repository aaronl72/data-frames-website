Why
Draws the name literally — four crop marks putting a frame around one piece of data. Corner marks read as print registration, not as a gunsight, which keeps it away from the shield-and-padlock cliché every other security firm uses.

Tradeoff
The bracket-corner square is a known form. It is ownable in this palette and beside this serif, but it is not a shape nobody has drawn before.
