Compact, but still wider than tall Compact and square-ish, so it works as an avatar without changes.mdash; an avatar would need the brackets on their own.

Why
A frame that only exists at two corners — the eye closes the rectangle itself. Half the weight of a full border with the same framing effect, and the two colours meeting on the diagonal give it the hard contrast the strong marks in this category rely on.

Tradeoff
Corner brackets are close to the crop-mark idea you turned down in the first round — heavier and two-tone here, but the same family. Opposing corners can also look unresolved to some people rather than deliberate.
