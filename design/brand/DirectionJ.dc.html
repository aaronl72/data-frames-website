The two halves keep it legible even when the seams close up.

Why
The frame idea with actual mass behind it: four blades closing on one opening, split hard down the diagonal. Solid all the way out to the edge, so there is nothing thin left to lose, and the two-tone split gives it the contrast Kaspersky and McAfee both trade on.

Tradeoff
The most decorative mark of the eight, and a square frame is a well-trodden shape. It only works while the two colours stay far apart — in one colour it collapses into a plain border.
