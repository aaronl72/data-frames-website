Solid block, so it never thins out. Strongest of the four at 16px.

Why
This is the McAfee and Malwarebytes move: a solid block of colour with the shape cut out of it rather than drawn in line. All the weight the first four were missing, and it holds its own next to a red Kaspersky or Qualys mark in a vendor list.

Tradeoff
A rounded green tile is also what every app icon looks like. It earns its keep through the cut-out, so the frame has to be drawn exactly right or it turns into another generic square.
