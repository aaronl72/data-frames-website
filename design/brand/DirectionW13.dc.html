The line flattens to a rule small — still reads, loses the story.

Why
A rule under the name, except it does not stay flat — it runs level under “Data” and starts climbing under “Frames”. The device is a trend line and a baseline at once, so the graphic element is load-bearing rather than decorative, and it is the only board in the round that says what the business is for. It also gives you a piece that can travel on its own: the line alone works as a divider across the site and the deck.

Tradeoff
An upward line is the single most used idea in business graphics, and it edges towards the growth-chart cliché that belongs to accountants and SaaS dashboards rather than to security. It also implies a promise you would have to keep — a rising line means results, and results in this field are measured in things not happening.
