The eyes hold to 16px. Below that the beak is the first to go.

Why
The other lesson from the category: the brands people genuinely remember use a creature, not an abstraction — CrowdStrike has a falcon, Snyk a dog. An owl is the honest one for what you sell. It watches all night and it does not miss much. Solid silhouette, so it carries the same weight as the rest.

Tradeoff
The most polarising of the eight. A creature either charms people or reads as soft for a firm that breaks into networks for a living, and there is not much middle ground. At a glance some people will read it as a cat before an owl. It is also the one that would need the most drawing to finish properly.
