The break over the top edge still reads at 16px. That is the whole mark.

Why
A frame with a trend line in it that refuses to stay inside — it climbs and breaks straight out over the top moulding. The only mark of the eleven with movement in it, and the escape is a useful thing to say in security: what matters is the thing that leaves the frame.

Tradeoff
A rising line is finance-chart language, and some people will read it as growth or investment rather than detection. The break-out is also fragile — crop it square for an avatar and you lose the entire idea.
