Why
W3 put a rule above the name and W7 put one between the words. This one runs the line straight through the letters — the name cut at the waist, the top half standing on light ground and the bottom half falling into dark. It is the most aggressive mark of the sixteen and the only one with real tension in it, and it earns the frame idea honestly: the horizon line is inside the picture rather than around it.

Tradeoff
It is a trick, and tricks age. The seam has to be redrawn as outlines for anything that is not a screen — it cannot be embroidered, engraved or faxed, and a single-colour version does not exist. It also dies earlier than anything else here: by 105px wide the cut is chewing the crossbars of the A and the E.
