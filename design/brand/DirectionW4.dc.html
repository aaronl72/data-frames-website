The three bars merge into one green blob under about 130px.

Why
The hyphen in your name is a free slot, and this puts the data in it. No separate symbol to place, no lockup rules to follow — the mark and the name are the same object, and the three bars can lift out on their own as an app icon when you need one.

Tradeoff
It is a joke that only works once you have read it, and it depends on people knowing the name has a hyphen at all. The bars also blur together at small sizes, which is exactly where a logo has to work hardest.
