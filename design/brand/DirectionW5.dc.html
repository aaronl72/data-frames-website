Why
You already own this device — the italic green accent is how your hero headline works today. Promoting it to the logo costs nothing to introduce, because every visitor has already seen the brand speak this way. No border, no symbol, no lockup rules.

Tradeoff
It is the quietest option of the eight, and quiet is the thing you objected to first. Instrument Serif is also a delicate face — below about 100px wide the thin strokes start to break up, and it has no square version.
