Why
Single-letter marks are the most common form in the category for a reason — Kaspersky's K, McAfee's M, Qualys' Q, Trend Micro's t. Two colours build the letter here rather than decorate it — the stem is one, the bowl the other — which is the move that gives those marks their hard contrast.

Tradeoff
It says nothing about what you do, and it drops half your name — there is no “Frames” in a D. It also puts you in the most crowded corner of the category.
