There is no 16px version. A wordmark cannot be a favicon.

Why
No symbol at all. Set heavy and tight in the sans you already use, with a solid bar doing the hyphen's job and locking the two words into one block. Nothing to misread, nothing to explain, and it is the one option here that cannot look like anyone else's app icon.

Tradeoff
Two real costs. You still need a separate small mark for favicons, avatars and anything square. And the logo would be in Plex Sans while your headlines are Instrument Serif — that split needs handling, or the site starts to feel like two brands.
