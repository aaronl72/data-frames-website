Reversed type stops being readable below about 120px wide.

Why
A nameplate. The name knocked out of a solid slab, with the hyphen doing its job as a green bar. It has the most physical presence of the eight — it reads as something screwed to a door rather than typed on a screen, which suits a firm people let into their building.

Tradeoff
A solid dark slab is heavy on a page and awkward on any background that is not plain. Reversed type also loses legibility faster than positive type as it shrinks.
